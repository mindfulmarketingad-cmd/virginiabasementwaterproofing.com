    What causes poor yard and foundation drainage in Chester?

    Chester sits in the Greater Richmond region, where the main driver is clay-heavy Piedmont soil combined with mature tree roots common in older neighborhoods. Roots seeking moisture can crack footings and clog drain tile, while clay swelling adds constant pressure against basement walls.

    How long does french drain installation take in Chester?

    1–2 days for interior drain tile; exterior drains can take longer depending on the run length. Your matched contractor will confirm an exact schedule during the free on-site estimate.

    Is french drain installation covered by a warranty in Chester?

    Interior drain tile is commonly warrantied for the life of the system when paired with a sump pump.
