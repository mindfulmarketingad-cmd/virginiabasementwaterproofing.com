    What causes basement flooding in Arlington?

    Arlington sits in the Northern Virginia region, where the main driver is heavy clay soil that expands when wet and shrinks when dry. That cycle stresses foundation walls every season, and the region's dense, older housing stock means many foundations were built before modern drainage codes existed.
