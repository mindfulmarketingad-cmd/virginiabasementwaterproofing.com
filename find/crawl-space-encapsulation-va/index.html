    Common Questions

    How much does crawl space encapsulation cost in Virginia?

    Typical cost runs $1,500–$8,000 depending on square footage and whether a dehumidifier is included statewide. Prices vary by region and by how much excavation or structural work is involved — the only accurate number comes from a free on-site inspection.

    How many crawl space encapsulation contractors are listed in Virginia?

    We currently track 40 contractors offering crawl space encapsulation across 29 Virginia cities and towns. Of the 39 with a public Google rating, the average is 4.8★.

    What causes crawl space moisture problems across Virginia?

    Mostly Virginia's mix of clay-heavy soil and a high water table in many areas. Clay expands when saturated and contracts when dry, and that seasonal cycle puts steady pressure on foundation walls and footings statewide. The specifics vary by region — coastal Hampton Roads deals with a high water table, while the Shenandoah Valley's limestone bedrock creates its own drainage quirks.     Is crawl space encapsulation covered by a warranty?

    Vapor barrier and encapsulation warranties commonly run 15–25 years and are usually transferable.
