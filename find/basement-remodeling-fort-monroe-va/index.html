    What causes the basement moisture issues that block finishing a space in Fort Monroe?

    Fort Monroe sits in the Hampton Roads region, where the main driver is a high water table and sandy, low-lying coastal soil. Groundwater sits close to the surface for much of the year, so basements and crawl spaces here fight hydrostatic pressure almost constantly, and storm surge or nor'easters can push the table up further overnight.
