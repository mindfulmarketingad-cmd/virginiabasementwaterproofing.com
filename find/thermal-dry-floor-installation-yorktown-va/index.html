    What causes cold, damp subfloors in Yorktown?

    Yorktown sits in the Hampton Roads region, where the main driver is a high water table and sandy, low-lying coastal soil. Groundwater sits close to the surface for much of the year, so basements and crawl spaces here fight hydrostatic pressure almost constantly, and storm surge or nor'easters can push the table up further overnight.
