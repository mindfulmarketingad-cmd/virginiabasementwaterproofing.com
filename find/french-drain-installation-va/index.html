    French drains redirect groundwater before it reaches your foundation walls or basement floor. Interior perimeter systems capture seepage beneath the slab; exterior systems intercept water in the soil before it exerts hydrostatic pressure. Virginia's clay soils make proper French drain design critical — and our contractors specialize in it.

    Common Questions

    How much does french drain installation cost in Virginia?

    Typical cost runs $2,000–$6,500 for an interior system, more for a full exterior French drain statewide. Prices vary by region and by how much excavation or structural work is involved — the only accurate number comes from a free on-site inspection.

    How many french drain installation contractors are listed in Virginia?

    We currently track 39 contractors offering french drain installation across 31 Virginia cities and towns. Of the 39 with a public Google rating, the average is 4.8★.

    What causes poor yard and foundation drainage across Virginia?

    Mostly Virginia's mix of clay-heavy soil and a high water table in many areas. Clay expands when saturated and contracts when dry, and that seasonal cycle puts steady pressure on foundation walls and footings statewide. The specifics vary by region — coastal Hampton Roads deals with a high water table, while the Shenandoah Valley's limestone bedrock creates its own drainage quirks.     How long does french drain installation take?

    1–2 days for interior drain tile; exterior drains can take longer depending on the run length. Your matched contractor will confirm an exact schedule during the free on-site estimate.

    Is french drain installation covered by a warranty?

    Interior drain tile is commonly warrantied for the life of the system when paired with a sump pump.
