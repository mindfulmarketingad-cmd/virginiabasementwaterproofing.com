    What causes poor yard and foundation drainage in Portsmouth?

    Portsmouth sits in the Hampton Roads region, where the main driver is a high water table and sandy, low-lying coastal soil. Groundwater sits close to the surface for much of the year, so basements and crawl spaces here fight hydrostatic pressure almost constantly, and storm surge or nor'easters can push the table up further overnight.

    How long does french drain installation take in Portsmouth?

    1–2 days for interior drain tile; exterior drains can take longer depending on the run length. Your matched contractor will confirm an exact schedule during the free on-site estimate.

    Is french drain installation covered by a warranty in Portsmouth?

    Interior drain tile is commonly warrantied for the life of the system when paired with a sump pump.
