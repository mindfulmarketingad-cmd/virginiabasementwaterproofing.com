    What causes water damage in North Chesterfield?

    North Chesterfield sits in the Greater Richmond region, where the main driver is clay-heavy Piedmont soil combined with mature tree roots common in older neighborhoods. Roots seeking moisture can crack footings and clog drain tile, while clay swelling adds constant pressure against basement walls.
