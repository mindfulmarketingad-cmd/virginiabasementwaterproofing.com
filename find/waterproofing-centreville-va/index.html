    What causes wet basement problems in Centreville?

    Centreville sits in the Northern Virginia region, where the main driver is heavy clay soil that expands when wet and shrinks when dry. That cycle stresses foundation walls every season, and the region's dense, older housing stock means many foundations were built before modern drainage codes existed.

    1–3 days for an interior system; exterior work can take a week or more because it involves excavation. Your matched contractor will confirm an exact schedule during the free on-site estimate.
