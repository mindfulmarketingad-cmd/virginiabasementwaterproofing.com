    What causes wet basement problems in Hardy?

    Hardy sits in the Western Virginia region, where the main driver is steep terrain and rocky, mountainous soil. Water moves fast downhill toward foundations built into hillsides, and older homes in the region often have minimal exterior drainage to intercept it.
