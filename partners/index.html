How We Vet Our Contractors

We want every Virginia homeowner to hire with confidence. Before a company appears in our in-network directory, we review the following:

  • Licensing: Verification of an active Virginia contractor license (Class A, B, or C as applicable).
  • Insurance: Proof of current general liability and, where required, workers' compensation coverage.
  • Reputation: A review of public ratings and customer feedback for a consistent track record.
  • Workmanship & warranties: Confirmation that the company stands behind its work with clear warranty terms.

Even with our screening, we always encourage homeowners to independently verify a contractor's license through the Virginia Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation (DPOR), request proof of insurance, and obtain multiple written estimates before signing any contract.
