About E 37 Waterproofing

E 37 Waterproofing is a Concrete contractor listed in our Virginia contractor directory, based in Bedford. The company works in concrete repair and resurfacing and serves homeowners throughout Bedford and the neighbouring communities.

Core work covers concrete repair and resurfacing and plumbing and drainage solutions, basement and crawl space waterproofing, alongside the interior and exterior drainage, sump pump, encapsulation and crack-repair work that Virginia's clay soils and high water table routinely demand. Any reputable contractor should start with an on-site inspection and give you a written, itemised estimate before work begins — ask E 37 Waterproofing for both.

Virginia basements fail for predictable reasons: hydrostatic pressure against below-grade walls, footing drains that have silted up, downspouts discharging against the foundation, and grading that slopes toward the house rather than away from it. A durable fix addresses the water source, not just the damp patch on the wall. When you compare quotes, compare the diagnosis first.

Before You Hire Any Contractor

  • Confirm the licence number with the Virginia DPOR and ask for a current certificate of insurance.
  • Get the diagnosis in writing — where the water is coming from, not just what will be installed.
  • Compare at least two itemised quotes covering the same scope of work.
  • Ask what the warranty covers, how long it runs, and whether it transfers when you sell.
  • Check the most recent reviews, not the lifetime average — crews and ownership change.
