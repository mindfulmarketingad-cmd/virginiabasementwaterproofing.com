Virginia basements fail for predictable reasons: hydrostatic pressure against below-grade walls, footing drains that have silted up, downspouts discharging against the foundation, and grading that slopes toward the house rather than away from it. Interior drainage systems typically run $3,000–$9,000, exterior waterproofing $8,000–$15,000 or more, sump pump installation $1,000–$3,500, and crack injection $500–$1,500 per crack. These are informational ranges, not quotes — the only accurate number comes from an on-site inspection.

Before You Hire Any Contractor

  • Confirm the licence number with the Virginia DPOR and ask for a current certificate of insurance.
  • Get the diagnosis in writing — where the water is coming from, not just what will be installed.
  • Compare at least two itemised quotes covering the same scope of work.
  • Ask what the warranty covers, how long it runs, and whether it transfers when you sell.
  • Check the most recent reviews, not the lifetime average — crews and ownership change.
