About Tidewater Dry Basements

Tidewater Dry Basements is a family-owned waterproofing company specializing in the unique coastal conditions of the Hampton Roads region. With more than two decades of experience keeping Virginia Beach and Norfolk homes dry, the team focuses on durable, warrantied solutions tailored to high-water-table environments.

Services Offered

  • Interior & exterior basement waterproofing
  • Crawl space encapsulation & dehumidification
  • Sump pump installation with battery backup
  • Foundation crack repair & wall reinforcement
  • French drains & yard drainage
  • Mold & moisture remediation

Service Area

Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Suffolk, Portsmouth, and surrounding Hampton Roads communities.

Credentials

  • General liability & workers' compensation insured
  • Transferable lifetime warranty on installed systems
  • Free, no-obligation written estimates
