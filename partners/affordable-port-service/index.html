About Affordable Port Service

Affordable Port Service is a licensed basement waterproofing and foundation services company based in Portsmouth, Virginia. The company specializes in water damage restoration and serves homeowners throughout Portsmouth and the neighboring communities. With a focus on long-term, warrantied solutions, Affordable Port Service helps Virginia families protect their homes from water intrusion, foundation moisture, and the structural damage that chronic dampness can cause.

The team at Affordable Port Service is trained to diagnose moisture problems at their source rather than simply treating the symptoms. Core service offerings include water damage restoration, interior and exterior drainage system installation, sump pump sales and installation, crawl space encapsulation with reinforced vapor barriers, and foundation crack repair using industry-standard injection and reinforcement methods. Every project begins with a thorough on-site inspection and a written, itemized estimate — no surprises, no pressure.

Affordable Port Service uses commercial-grade waterproofing materials sourced from leading manufacturers in the waterproofing industry. Products commonly installed include high-capacity sump pump systems with battery backup, reinforced polyethylene vapor barriers for crawl space encapsulation, interior drain tile systems, wall anchor and carbon fiber strap systems for bowing or leaning basement walls, epoxy and polyurethane injection kits for crack repair, and heavy-duty dehumidification units for humidity control. All products are selected for Virginia's climate and soil conditions.

Based in Portsmouth, Affordable Port Service serves residential and light-commercial clients throughout the Portsmouth area and beyond. The company is available for projects across a wide service radius and can accommodate urgent calls when active water intrusion or flooding threatens a home. Contact our network at (757) 743-9050 to confirm service availability in your specific ZIP code and schedule a free on-site consultation.

Choosing the right waterproofing contractor is one of the most important decisions a homeowner can make for the long-term value and safety of their property. Affordable Port Service combines local expertise, quality materials, and transparent pricing to deliver results that last.
