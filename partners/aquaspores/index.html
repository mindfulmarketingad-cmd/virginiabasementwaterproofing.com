About AquaSpores

AquaSpores is a licensed basement waterproofing and foundation services company based in Lynchburg, Virginia. The company specializes in water damage restoration and serves homeowners throughout Lynchburg and the neighboring communities. With a focus on long-term, warrantied solutions, AquaSpores helps Virginia families protect their homes from water intrusion, foundation moisture, and the structural damage that chronic dampness can cause. With a 5.0-star Google rating backed by 75 verified reviews, the company has built a reputation for dependable results and responsive customer service.

The team at AquaSpores is trained to diagnose moisture problems at their source rather than simply treating the symptoms. Core service offerings include water damage restoration, interior and exterior drainage system installation, sump pump sales and installation, crawl space encapsulation with reinforced vapor barriers, and foundation crack repair using industry-standard injection and reinforcement methods. Every project begins with a thorough on-site inspection and a written, itemized estimate — no surprises, no pressure.

AquaSpores uses commercial-grade waterproofing materials sourced from leading manufacturers in the waterproofing industry. Products commonly installed include high-capacity sump pump systems with battery backup, reinforced polyethylene vapor barriers for crawl space encapsulation, interior drain tile systems, wall anchor and carbon fiber strap systems for bowing or leaning basement walls, epoxy and polyurethane injection kits for crack repair, and heavy-duty dehumidification units for humidity control. All products are selected for Virginia's climate and soil conditions.

Based in Lynchburg, AquaSpores serves residential and light-commercial clients throughout the Lynchburg area and beyond. The company is available for projects across a wide service radius and can accommodate urgent calls when active water intrusion or flooding threatens a home. Choosing the right waterproofing contractor is one of the most important decisions a homeowner can make for the long-term value and safety of their property. AquaSpores combines local expertise, quality materials, and transparent pricing to deliver results that last.
