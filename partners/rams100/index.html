About RAMS100

RAMS100 is a licensed basement waterproofing and foundation services company based in Reston, Virginia. The company specializes in water damage restoration and serves homeowners throughout Reston and the neighboring communities. With a focus on long-term, warrantied solutions, RAMS100 helps Virginia families protect their homes from water intrusion, foundation moisture, and the structural damage that chronic dampness can cause. With a 5.0-star Google rating backed by 1 verified reviews, the company has built a reputation for dependable results and responsive customer service.

The team at RAMS100 is trained to diagnose moisture problems at their source rather than simply treating the symptoms. Core service offerings include water damage restoration, interior and exterior drainage system installation, sump pump sales and installation, crawl space encapsulation with reinforced vapor barriers, and foundation crack repair using industry-standard injection and reinforcement methods. Every project begins with a thorough on-site inspection and a written, itemized estimate — no surprises, no pressure.

RAMS100 uses commercial-grade waterproofing materials sourced from leading manufacturers in the waterproofing industry. Products commonly installed include high-capacity sump pump systems with battery backup, reinforced polyethylene vapor barriers for crawl space encapsulation, interior drain tile systems, wall anchor and carbon fiber strap systems for bowing or leaning basement walls, epoxy and polyurethane injection kits for crack repair, and heavy-duty dehumidification units for humidity control. All products are selected for Virginia's climate and soil conditions.

Based in Reston, RAMS100 serves residential and light-commercial clients throughout the Reston area and beyond. The company is available for projects across a wide service radius and can accommodate urgent calls when active water intrusion or flooding threatens a home.
