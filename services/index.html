Waterproofing & Foundation Services in Virginia

From minor seepage to chronic flooding, Virginia's licensed contractors offer a full range of solutions to keep your basement, crawl space, and foundation dry, healthy, and structurally sound.

Basement Waterproofing

Interior drainage systems, exterior membranes, and wall sealants that channel water away from your foundation and into a sump system — keeping your basement permanently dry.

Crawl Space Encapsulation

Heavy-duty vapor barriers, sealed vents, and commercial dehumidifiers create a clean, dry crawl space that protects your structure and improves indoor air quality.

Foundation Repair

Crack injection, carbon-fiber straps, wall anchors, and underpinning piers address bowing walls and settling foundations caused by Virginia's clay soils and hydrostatic pressure.

Sump Pump Installation

Primary and battery-backup sump pumps sized for Virginia's storm seasons — installed, maintained, and replaced by licensed local contractors.

French Drain Installation

Interior and exterior French drains, curtain drains, and downspout tie-ins that redirect groundwater and surface runoff away from your foundation.

Basement Crack Repair

Polyurethane and epoxy injection plus carbon-fiber reinforcement permanently seal leaking and structural cracks in poured concrete and block walls.

Basement Water Damage Restoration

Emergency water extraction, structural drying, mold prevention, and full rebuild services for Virginia basements affected by flooding, pipe bursts, or storm damage.

Basement Remodeling

Finish or transform your Virginia basement into livable space — framing, flooring, egress windows, bathrooms, and electrical by licensed local contractors.

Black Mold Treatment

Safe, certified mold testing, containment, removal, and remediation for Virginia basements and crawl spaces following EPA and IICRC S520 standards.

Choosing the Right Waterproofing Solution

Virginia's climate brings heavy seasonal rainfall, coastal hurricanes, and a wide range of soil types — from the dense clays of the Tidewater region to the rocky soils of the Blue Ridge. These conditions create persistent water pressure against foundations, leading to leaks, dampness, efflorescence, and over time, structural damage.

A professional waterproofing contractor begins with a thorough diagnosis to identify the water's source: surface runoff, a high water table, hydrostatic pressure, plumbing leaks, or condensation. The right solution depends on the cause, your foundation type, and your budget.
