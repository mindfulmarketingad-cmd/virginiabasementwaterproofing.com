Basement Waterproofing Services in Virginia

From minor seepage to chronic flooding, Virginia's licensed waterproofing contractors offer a full range of solutions to keep your basement and foundation dry, healthy, and structurally sound.

Interior Basement Waterproofing

Interior drainage systems, perimeter drain tile, and sealants channel water away from your foundation and into a sump system. Ideal for managing seepage without major excavation.

Exterior Waterproofing

Excavation to the foundation footing, waterproof membranes, and exterior drainage stop water before it ever reaches your basement walls.
⚙️

Sump Pump Installation

Primary and battery-backup sump pumps collect and discharge water automatically, protecting your basement even during power outages and severe storms.

French Drains & Drainage

Interior and exterior French drains, downspout extensions, and grading corrections redirect groundwater and surface runoff away from your home.
️

Crawl Space Encapsulation

Heavy-duty vapor barriers, sealed vents, and dehumidification create a clean, dry crawl space that improves air quality and reduces energy costs.

Foundation Repair

Crack injection, carbon-fiber reinforcement, wall anchors, and underpinning address bowing walls and structural movement caused by hydrostatic pressure.

Mold & Moisture Control

Humidity management, mold remediation, and antimicrobial treatments protect your family's health and prevent damage to stored belongings.

Free Inspections & Estimates

Most listed contractors provide a free, no-obligation inspection and written estimate so you understand the problem and your options before committing.

Choosing the Right Waterproofing Solution

Virginia's climate brings heavy seasonal rainfall, coastal hurricanes, and a wide range of soil types — from the dense clays of the Tidewater region to the rocky soils of the Blue Ridge. These conditions create persistent water pressure against foundations, leading to leaks, dampness, efflorescence, and over time, structural damage.

A professional waterproofing contractor begins with a thorough diagnosis to identify the water's source: surface runoff, a high water table, hydrostatic pressure, plumbing leaks, or condensation. The right solution depends on the cause, your foundation type, and your budget.
