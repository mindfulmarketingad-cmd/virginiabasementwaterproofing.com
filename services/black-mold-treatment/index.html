Black Mold Treatment in Virginia

Licensed local contractors serving homeowners across Virginia. Free estimates, no obligation.

Submit Job Request Call (757) 743-9050

Black mold — Stachybotrys chartarum — thrives in the damp, poorly ventilated conditions found in Virginia basements and crawl spaces. It grows on wood framing, drywall, insulation, and concrete surfaces that stay wet for more than 24 to 48 hours. Exposure can cause respiratory symptoms, headaches, and chronic health issues, particularly for children, the elderly, and anyone with allergies or asthma. Professional black mold treatment goes beyond surface cleaning: it identifies the moisture source, removes contaminated materials safely, treats structural surfaces with EPA-registered antimicrobials, and implements controls that prevent recurrence.

Black Mold Treatment Services We Provide:

Mold Testing & Air Sampling

Certified inspectors take surface swab and air samples to confirm mold species, measure spore counts, and define the scope of contamination before any work begins.

Containment & Safe Removal

Negative-pressure containment barriers isolate the work area. Contaminated drywall, insulation, and wood materials are removed following IICRC S520 remediation standards.

Structural Surface Treatment

Exposed framing, joists, concrete, and block are HEPA-vacuumed, scrubbed, and treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial and encapsulant products.

Moisture Source Correction

Remediation without fixing the moisture source always fails. Our contractors identify and correct the water intrusion — drainage, vapor barrier, waterproofing, or HVAC — that fed the mold.

Post-Remediation Clearance Testing

Independent air and surface testing after work is complete confirms that spore counts have returned to normal levels and the space is safe to reoccupy.
