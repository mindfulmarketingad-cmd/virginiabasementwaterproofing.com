Basement Remodeling in Virginia

Licensed local contractors serving homeowners across Virginia. Free estimates, no obligation.

Submit Job Request Call (757) 743-9050

An unfinished basement is one of the most underutilized assets in a Virginia home. Finishing or remodeling that space can add hundreds of square feet of livable area, increase resale value, and make your home work harder for your family. From framing and drywall to flooring, lighting, egress windows, and wet bars, Virginia's licensed basement remodeling contractors handle every phase of the project — ensuring the work meets code, passes inspection, and is built to last in our humid climate.

Basement Remodeling Services We Provide:

Basement Finishing & Framing

Full framing, insulation, drywall, and finishing that converts raw basement space into a conditioned, livable room ready for flooring and paint.

Flooring Installation

Moisture-resistant flooring options — luxury vinyl plank, engineered hardwood, tile, and carpet — selected and installed for below-grade conditions.

Egress Windows & Permits

Code-compliant egress window installation that makes basement bedrooms legal, improves natural light, and provides emergency exit access.

Bathroom & Wet Bar Rough-In

Plumbing rough-in and finish work for basement half-baths, full bathrooms, and wet bar areas including drain tie-in and fixture installation.

Lighting, Electrical & HVAC

Recessed lighting, outlet rough-in, panel upgrades, and HVAC extensions that make the finished basement comfortable year-round.
