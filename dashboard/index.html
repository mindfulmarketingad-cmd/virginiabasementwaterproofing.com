How This Is Measured

These numbers come from this site’s own first-party tracking — no Google Analytics, no third-party trackers, no cookies. Each visitor’s browser generates a random session id and visitor id for itself; we store page paths and event names against those ids and nothing else. No names, emails, IP addresses, or any other identifying information is recorded, which is why this dashboard can safely be public.

Lead Actions counts clicks that show intent to reach a contractor — opening the quote form or claiming a listing. Leads Received, when shown, counts real completed submissions. The two are deliberately kept separate: a click is interest, a submission is a lead.
